✦ A MESSAGE FROM THE HEART ✦

Dear Dad — Mahmoud EL-Garhy,

I write these words with a heavy heart, knowing that a whole week has slipped by in silence between us — a silence entirely of my own making, and one I am truly, deeply sorry for.

Life carried me along in its current, but that is no excuse. No deadline, no task, no passing hour is ever worth more than the sound of your voice or the warmth of simply knowing we are connected.

You have given me everything — your time, your wisdom, your endless patience, and a love so steady it has never once wavered. The very least I can offer in return is my presence, and I failed at that this week. For that, I am sorry, Baba.

I promise to do better. Not just in grand gestures, but in the small, faithful act of picking up the phone and saying “I was just thinking of you.” Because I am — always.

Please forgive your son, who is still learning, still growing, and who is endlessly grateful to have you as his father.

With all my love and respect,
Your Engineer ❤️

I Love You, Dad 💌
